Our Mission.

CampusConnect Foundation is a student-led organisation dedicated to improving communication between students, lecturers, Representative Council of Learners (RCLs) and campus management. We provide an easy-to-use online platform where students can submit concerns, ask questions, receive campus updates and access important support services.

About CampusConnect Foundation

CampusConnect Foundation was established to create better communication between students and campus management. Through one central online platform, students can report concerns, share suggestions, receive announcements and connect with the support they need.

About Us

Our Story

How CampusConnect began

CampusConnect Foundation was founded in 2026 by Sinoxolo Tyeke, a student at Rosebank College's Nelson Mandela Bay campus, after seeing how difficult it was for students to get their concerns heard.

With only a handful of RCL representatives covering an entire faculty, requests and complaints were often delayed or missed, and many students felt uneasy approaching lecturers or campus management directly. Most communication happened through scattered WhatsApp groups and emails.

Seeing this gap, Sino started CampusConnect as a student-led initiative to build one central online platform where students could submit concerns, suggestions and requests directly to the RCL, lecturers and campus management.

Our Mission

To give students an easy, safe online space to share problems, get information, get help and connect with their campus quickly.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading student support organisation by empowering students through accessible, transparent and innovative communication.

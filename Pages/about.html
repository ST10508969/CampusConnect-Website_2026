About CampusConnect Foundation

Empowering students through communication, support and collaboration.

Our Story

CampusConnect Foundation was established in 2026 by Sinoxolo Tyeke after identifying communication challenges between students, lecturers and campus management. The organisation aims to provide a single online platform where students can submit concerns, receive updates, access support services and stay informed.

Our Mission

To assist students by providing an easy online platform where they can share concerns, access information, receive support and connect with their institution.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading student support organisation through accessible, transparent and innovative communication solutions.
